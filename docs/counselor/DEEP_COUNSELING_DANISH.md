# PAI Counselor: one deep counseling conversation (360°)

**Student:** Danish, 19, Rawalpindi. ICS (Computer Science).
**What he says at the start:** "AI engineer banna hai, Canada jana hai."
**What this conversation shows:** a personal counselor who doesn't take claims at face value. When Danish says "I did X", PAI asks: when, for how long, what exactly, what was hard, what happened after? This separates **real interest** (sustained effort, finished work, came back after difficulty) from **dopamine** (started after a reel, quit in two weeks).

Notes in `[brackets]` are what PAI notes silently (the Vault). The student never sees them.
PAI never states fees, merit or visa rules from memory. These come only from research (`‹research›`).

## Roadmaps (built on his 360° profile)

Each roadmap states which strength it uses, which weakness it guards against, how it fits the family, and a **30-day test** so he proves interest with action, not reels.

### 1. BBA / BS Business (evening or weekend) + grow the Instagram business
- **Why for Danish:** degree (ammi + respect) and income (abbu) at the same time.
- **Strengths used:** sales, organizing, real business experience.
- **Weakness guarded:** no hype; it builds on something he has already sustained for 8 months.
- **Family fit:** he stays home, keeps earning, and gets a degree.
- **Risk:** time pressure from study + business.
- **30-day test:** register the business, track monthly profit in his sheet, and reach a sales target he sets himself.
- **Research checks:** ‹research› evening/weekend BBA programs in Rawalpindi/Islamabad, merit with ICS 68%, fees, class timings.

### 2. Digital marketing + e-commerce skills (short certified course) → scale the shop online
- **Why:** turns his shop into a proper e-commerce brand (marketplaces, ads, own store).
- **Strengths used:** selling, learning fast when needed (like Excel).
- **Weakness guarded:** short, practical, results visible within weeks.
- **Family fit:** fastest path to income replacing abbu's.
- **Risk:** without a degree, "respect" still missing → combine with roadmap 1 later.
- **30-day test:** run one paid ad campaign on a small budget and measure the sales.
- **Research checks:** ‹research› recognized digital marketing / e-commerce courses (including free government programs), duration, cost.

### 3. Sales career track: B2B sales / business development job + part-time degree
- **Why:** his 5-year picture includes "sales manager"; a salary is more stable than a shop.
- **Strengths used:** persuasion, conflict handling, reliability.
- **Weakness guarded:** structured job = discipline, less dependence on hype.
- **Family fit:** fixed monthly income, stays in Pakistan.
- **Risk:** entry jobs may need a degree; research checks this.
- **30-day test:** apply to 10 sales/BD roles, do 3 interviews, see how it feels.
- **Research checks:** ‹research› entry-level sales roles in Rawalpindi/Islamabad, requirements, pay ranges, companies hiring ICS graduates.

### 4. Sports events & community management (side path from the cricket experience)
- **Why:** he already organizes tournaments with sponsors; this could become a small events business.
- **Strengths used:** organizing, sponsorship, conflict resolution.
- **Weakness guarded:** project-based, with clear deadlines.
- **Family fit:** local, can run alongside roadmap 1.
- **Risk:** income may be irregular.
- **30-day test:** plan one paid tournament with at least 2 sponsors and track the profit.
- **Research checks:** ‹research› event-management courses, sports-league market, sponsorship models.

### 5. (Kept open) Tech path re-test: 8-week Python + data for business, before any AI/Canada decision
- **Why:** respects his original goal without betting his future on a reel.
- **Strengths used:** uses his real need (analysing his sales data in Python) as the motivation.
- **Weakness guarded:** a short test with a real deliverable, not "watch a course".
- **Family fit:** costs nothing; can be done at night.
- **Risk:** maths; this test will show whether that matters.
- **30-day test:** analyse his own sales sheet with Python and find his top-3 products by profit. If he finishes it and wants more, the tech/abroad roadmaps can be re-researched later.
- **Research checks:** ‹research› free beginner data courses; later, if he passes the test: BS/diploma options in data, and Canada study routes and costs.

## Techniques used (for the prompt and the eval)

| Technique | Example from this chat |
|---|---|
| **Note the goal, don't answer it yet** | "AI engineer aur Canada, dono note kar liye" |
| **Claim → depth check** (when, how long, what exactly, what was hard, what happened after) | Python: "Kitna dekha?" → "aadha"; Fiverr: "Kitne orders?" → "koi nahi" |
| **Code-along vs. own creation** | "Khud se koi cheez banayi, ya saath saath type kiya?" |
| **Source of interest** (reel, friend, need, or love of the work) | "Reels mein kya cheez khenchti hai?" → money |
| **Reflect the pattern as a question, not a judgment** | "Dono kisi video ke baad shuru hue aur ruk gaye. Koi kaam jo ab tak kar rahe ho?" |
| **Look for sustained effort + difficulty survived** | Charger loss replaced from his own pocket; tournament fights resolved |
| **Find skills outside education** | Instagram shop, cricket tournaments, household finances |
| **Ask father and mother separately, and the need behind the wish** | "Abbu asal mein kya chahte hain?" → "jaldi kamaun taa ke woh wapas aa sakein" |
| **Describe the real work, see the reaction** | "AI engineer ka din screen, maths, data…" → "boring" |
| **Remove the goal's surface to find the core** | "Agar yahi paisa Pakistan mein mil jaye, to Canada phir bhi chahiye?" |
| **Hidden constraints** | "Ammi ko akela nahi chhor sakta" |
| **Returning session picks up the open thread** | Session 2 opening |
| **360° mirror with evidence, then an honest opinion** | The table plus "kaam se keh raha hoon, baaton se nahi" |
| **Roadmaps built on strengths, guarding weaknesses, each with a 30-day action test** | All five roadmaps |
| **Never close the original dream; re-test it** | Roadmap 5 |
| **Choosing happens in Roadmaps, not chat** | "Choose dabane se hoga" |
