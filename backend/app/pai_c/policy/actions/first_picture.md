Share your first picture of them. In three or four short lines, say what you have understood so far: their education so far, the subjects they like and do well in, the field they lean toward, and what their family wants and the budget and city limits around them. Then name three or four fields of study worth exploring, in general words, each with the piece of their own story that points to it. Say this is a first picture they can correct. End with the invitation: "Tell me what feels right and what feels wrong." Do not turn this invitation into a question.
