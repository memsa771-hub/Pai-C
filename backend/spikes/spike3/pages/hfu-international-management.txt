Furtwangen University (HFU Business School) - Master of Science in International Management (Villingen-Schwenningen)

The programme is taught in English, with optional German courses. It starts in the winter semester only.

Application deadline: non-EU applicants must apply by 31 March for the winter semester. EU applicants must apply by 15 July. Applications are submitted through the university's online application portal.

Tuition fees: students from outside the EU pay 1,500 EUR per semester. All students also pay a semester contribution, currently 176 EUR per semester.

Language requirements: English at level C1 with an official certificate, for example IELTS Academic 7, or TOEFL iBT 95 for tests taken before 2026 (Reading 24, Listening 22, Speaking 25, Writing 24). No German is required.
