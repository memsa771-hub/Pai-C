Saarland University - Visual Computing, Master of Science (Saarbruecken)

The programme is taught in English.

Application deadline: for the winter semester, the early deadline is 15 November, with admission letters by mid-February, and the regular deadline is 15 May, with decisions in August. For the summer semester, the early deadline is 15 May, with admission letters by mid-July, and the regular deadline is 15 November, with decisions in January.

Tuition fees: none. Students pay a semester contribution of 394.30 EUR per semester, covering a public transport ticket, insurance and access to university facilities.

Language requirements: English at level C1 with an official certificate, for example IELTS Academic 7, PTE Academic 76, or TOEFL iBT 95 for tests taken before 2026 (Reading 24, Listening 22, Speaking 25, Writing 24). No German is required.
