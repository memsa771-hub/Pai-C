Technical University of Munich - Informatics (Computer Science), Master of Science (Garching near Munich)

The programme is taught in English.

Application deadline: applicants from all countries apply by 31 May for the winter semester and by 30 November for the summer semester. Applications are submitted through the TUM online application portal. Applicants with a bachelor's degree from outside Germany must also provide preliminary documentation from uni-assist.

Tuition fees: they vary per semester and depend on several factors. The listing gives no amount and refers to the TUM tuition fees page. The semester contribution amount is also not stated in the listing.

Language requirements: English at level C1 with an official certificate, for example IELTS Academic 6.5, PTE Academic 65, or TOEFL iBT 88 for tests taken before 2026. No German is required. Applicants with a degree from Bangladesh, China, India, Iran or Pakistan must also submit GRE or GATE scores.
