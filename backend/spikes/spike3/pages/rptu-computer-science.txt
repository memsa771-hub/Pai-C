RPTU University Kaiserslautern-Landau - Computer Science, Master of Science (Kaiserslautern)

The programme is taught in English and starts in the winter or summer semester.

Application deadline: it depends on visa status. Applicants from abroad who need a visa must apply by 30 April for the winter semester and by 31 October for the summer semester. Applicants who already hold a visa, and applicants from the EU, must apply by 15 July for the winter semester and by 15 January for the summer semester. Applications are submitted through the programme's own application website.

Tuition fees: none. Students pay a social fee of 350 EUR per semester. Verifying foreign certificates costs a one-off assessment fee of 60 EUR.

Language requirements: English at level B2 with an official certificate, for example IELTS Academic 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 81 for tests taken before 2026. German at level A1 is recommended, but no proof is required. The GRE is strongly recommended.
