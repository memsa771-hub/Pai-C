Kiel University of Applied Sciences - Computer Science, Master of Science

The programme is taught entirely in English and lasts three semesters (90 credit points). It can be studied full-time or part-time and starts in the winter or summer semester.

Application deadline: applications go through uni-assist. Applicants from all countries apply between 1 May and 15 July for the winter semester, and between 1 November and 15 January for the summer semester.

Tuition fees: none. Students pay a semester contribution of 397.70 EUR per semester, which can vary and includes a public transport ticket.

Language requirements: English at level B2 with an official certificate, for example IELTS Academic 5.5. No German is required. Applicants whose degree is not from a Bologna-system country also need a GRE score of at least 55% in quantitative reasoning.
